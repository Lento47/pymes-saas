# Platform Overview

## Purpose

Provide a public-facing summary of how PymesHub combines customer operations,
billing, documentation, and workspace governance for multi-team companies.

## Core Areas

- Omnichannel customer operations with shared ownership and handoffs
- Revenue workflows that connect conversations, invoices, and follow-up
- Workspace governance through roles, visibility, and operational controls
- Documentation and trust references that support rollout and procurement

## Intended Audience

- Prospects evaluating the product before rollout
- Clients preparing internal buy-in
- Security, procurement, and operations stakeholders
