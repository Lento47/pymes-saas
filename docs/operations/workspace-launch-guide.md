# Workspace Launch Guide

## Purpose

Give new teams a public checklist for launching a PymesHub workspace with clear
roles, channels, and operating expectations.

## Launch Checklist

- Confirm workspace owner, admins, and day-to-day operators
- Decide which channels and customer flows go live first
- Review support, SLA, privacy, and legal references before launch
- Align the initial workflow for conversations, billing, and follow-up

## Intended Audience

- New customers preparing for rollout
- Internal onboarding and customer success teams
