### The software and its licence

- **PymesHub's own software is published under the MIT licence**, with the copyright notice **© 2026 Otnel**. The full text ships with the repository as `LICENSE`.
- In practice MIT means you may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense and sell copies of the software, provided the copyright notice and the permission notice travel with it — and that the software is provided **without warranty**.
- **Third-party open-source components keep their own licences.** They are credited, with their licences, on the open-source credits page.
- **The licence covers the software, not the service.** Having the code under MIT does not grant access to our accounts, our data, our hosting, our brand or anyone's marketplace account. Using the platform as a customer, business or courier is governed by the Legal Center documents.

### Who may use it

- **People aged 18 or older.** Creating an account requires confirming your age and accepting the terms, and that confirmation is recorded.
- **Businesses**, through a person who is authorised to commit that business.
- Anyone acting lawfully: the platform may not be used for unlawful goods, unlawful conduct, or to process other people's data without a legal basis.
- **Not for minors**, and not for holding children's data.

### What you may do

- Buy, sell and deliver on the marketplace, with your own catalogue, prices, hours and delivery settings.
- Publish your own shop, respond to reviews, run promotions, invite your team, invite verified couriers.
- Report bugs, send ideas, and ask for support.
- Run the open-source code yourself, within the terms of its licence, including for your own purposes.

### What you may not do

The complete and binding list is the **Acceptable Use Policy** in the Legal Center. In plain terms, it covers: scraping or bulk-extracting the catalogue or other people's data; creating accounts in bulk or impersonating someone; bypassing plan limits, rate limits or payment rules; interfering with other users' orders, deliveries or accounts; using delivery location data for anything other than the delivery; uploading unlawful, infringing or malicious content; sending spam through the platform; and probing or degrading the service (denial-of-service or stress testing is out of scope even for security research).

### Accounts, roles and access

- One person, one account. Credentials are yours to protect; tell us immediately if you suspect they were used by someone else.
- What a person may do inside a business is set by their **role**, and the invitation is the only way in — a business cannot add an account without the person accepting.
- A courier is an independent account that a business invites; a courier is not an employee of Otnel or of the business.
- An account may be suspended for abuse, for a security risk, or where the law requires it. The Terms describe the process; the data and privacy page describes what happens to data.

PymesHub is in **closed beta and early access**, and the Terms say so plainly:

- features may change, be limited, be suspended temporarily or contain errors;
- the service is provided **as is** and **as available**, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability;
- metrics, alerts and projections shown in the product are referential, and they are not financial, legal or tax advice;
- paid plans are published with their prices, and a future price change applies as a **new price book** — a subscription keeps the book it joined under.

- Una cuenta puede suspenderse por abuso, por riesgo de seguridad o cuando la ley lo exija. Los Términos describen el proceso; la página de datos y privacidad describe qué pasa con los datos.
